Help Center

How does TabMeter track my time?
TabMeter records time only while a tab is actively focused and Chrome reports you as active (not idle). The timer pauses automatically when you switch tabs, minimize the browser, or go AFK.
Is my data sent to any server?
No. All data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's storage API. Nothing is ever uploaded or shared externally. Your browsing habits stay completely private.
What is the Idle Timeout setting?
Idle Timeout controls how long TabMeter waits before pausing the timer when you stop interacting with Chrome. The default is 60 seconds. You can adjust it in Settings → Tracking → Idle Timeout.
How do daily limits work?
You can set a daily time limit for any website. Once you reach the limit, TabMeter will block the site for the rest of the day. Limits reset at midnight. Hover over a site in the list and click the timer icon to set a limit.
What is the Side Panel view?
Side Panel mode opens TabMeter as a persistent sidebar inside your browser instead of a small popup. Great for keeping an eye on your stats while browsing. Switch between modes in Settings → Display.
How do I export or clear my data?
Go to Settings → Data. Use "Export" to download a JSON backup of all your tracking history. Use "Delete" to permanently erase all data. Deleted data cannot be recovered.
